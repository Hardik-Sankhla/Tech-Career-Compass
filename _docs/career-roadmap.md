## Understanding Tech Career Progression

Tech careers follow general patterns, though timelines vary by individual and role. This guide outlines typical progression paths and what to expect at each stage.

## Career Levels Overview

### Entry Level (Junior) - Years 0-2
**Focus:** Learning fundamentals and building confidence

**Characteristics:**
- Heavy guidance and mentorship needed
- Focus on mastering basic skills
- Small, well-defined tasks
- Frequent code reviews and feedback

**Key Milestones:**
- Ship first major feature
- Complete first on-call rotation (if applicable)
- Navigate codebase independently
- Debug production issues with guidance

**Typical Compensation:** $70,000 - $130,000

### Mid-Level (IC2/IC3) - Years 3-5
**Focus:** Independence and ownership

**Characteristics:**
- Work independently on features
- Mentor junior developers
- Lead small projects
- Make technical decisions for your domain

**Key Milestones:**
- Design systems from scratch
- Lead project end-to-end
- Influence team technical direction
- Resolve complex production issues

**Typical Compensation:** $100,000 - $175,000

### Senior (IC4) - Years 6-10
**Focus:** Technical leadership and impact

**Characteristics:**
- Drive architecture decisions
- Mentor team members
- Set technical standards
- Balance technical depth and breadth

**Key Milestones:**
- Design multi-team systems
- Drive technical initiatives
- Resolve ambiguous problems
- Influence org-wide decisions

**Typical Compensation:** $140,000 - $250,000+

### Staff+ (IC5+) - Years 10+
**Focus:** Organizational impact

**Characteristics:**
- Set technical vision
- Influence company direction
- Solve hardest technical problems
- Multiply team effectiveness

**Key Milestones:**
- Lead company-wide initiatives
- Establish engineering practices
- Navigate complex tradeoffs
- Mentor senior engineers

**Typical Compensation:** $200,000 - $500,000+

## Two Career Tracks

### Individual Contributor (IC) Track
**Focus on:** Technical depth, architecture, solving hard problems

**Progression:**
Junior → Mid → Senior → Staff → Principal → Distinguished

**Best for:**
- Deep technical interest
- Solving complex problems
- Architecture and design
- Technical mentorship

### Management Track
**Focus on:** People leadership, team growth, strategic planning

**Progression:**
Senior IC → Engineering Manager → Senior EM → Director → VP → CTO

**Best for:**
- People management
- Building teams
- Strategic planning
- Cross-functional leadership

## Salary Growth Patterns

### Typical Progression
- **Years 0-2:** $70K → $100K (40% growth)
- **Years 3-5:** $100K → $140K (40% growth)
- **Years 6-8:** $140K → $200K (40% growth)
- **Years 9-12:** $200K → $300K+ (50% growth)

### Acceleration Factors
- **Job hopping:** 20-30% increases every 2-3 years
- **Promotions:** 10-25% increases
- **Specialization:** 10-20% premium for hot skills
- **Company growth:** Equity value appreciation
- **Location:** 30-50% variance by city

### Deceleration Factors
- Staying at same company too long
- Not keeping skills updated
- Avoiding leadership opportunities
- Poor negotiation skills
- Geographic limitations

## Promotion Timelines

### Healthy Progression
- **Junior → Mid:** 2-3 years
- **Mid → Senior:** 3-4 years
- **Senior → Staff:** 4-6 years
- **Staff → Principal:** 5+ years

### Red Flags
- Promotion promised but delayed 2+ years
- No clear promotion criteria
- "You're almost there" for multiple cycles
- Company has no senior+ ICs
- No growth plan or feedback

### Accelerated Progression
Possible through:
- Exceptional performance
- High-growth startups
- Taking on critical projects
- Filling organizational gaps
- Strong sponsors/mentors

## Career Transitions

### Lateral Moves
**When to consider:**
- Learning new technologies
- Changing specializations
- Better company culture
- Improved work-life balance

**Salary impact:** 0-15% increase typical

### Upward Moves
**When to pursue:**
- Ready for more responsibility
- Demonstrated higher-level work
- Strong performance record
- Promoted vs. job hopping decision

**Salary impact:** 10-30% increase typical

### Company Switches
**Optimal timing:**
- Every 2-3 years for salary growth
- After major project completion
- When learning has plateaued
- Better opportunities available

**Salary impact:** 15-40% increase typical

## Building Towards Next Level

### Promotion Strategy

**1. Understand Requirements**
- Get written promotion criteria
- Study peers at target level
- Identify skill gaps
- Create development plan

**2. Gather Evidence**
- Document your impact
- Collect peer feedback
- Demonstrate target-level work
- Build promotion packet

**3. Communicate Progress**
- Regular 1:1s with manager
- Share wins and learnings
- Ask for stretch assignments
- Seek sponsor support

**4. Be Patient but Persistent**
- Expect 1-2 year timelines
- Repeatedly demonstrate capability
- Don't wait indefinitely
- Know when to move on

### Skills to Prioritize

**Universal Skills:**
- Communication (written & verbal)
- System design and architecture
- Debugging and problem-solving
- Mentoring and leadership
- Business understanding

**Role-Specific Skills:**
- See individual career path guides
- Focus on high-leverage skills
- Specialize strategically
- Stay current with technology

## Common Career Mistakes

### Early Career (Years 0-5)
❌ Job hopping too frequently (every year)
❌ Not asking for help
❌ Neglecting soft skills
❌ Avoiding difficult projects
❌ Not building a portfolio

✅ Stay 2-3 years to learn deeply
✅ Ask questions and seek mentorship
✅ Practice communication
✅ Take on challenges
✅ Document your work

### Mid Career (Years 6-12)
❌ Staying at same company too long
❌ Not specializing
❌ Avoiding leadership
❌ Ignoring industry trends
❌ Poor salary negotiation

✅ Strategic job hopping
✅ Develop deep expertise
✅ Mentor and lead
✅ Continuous learning
✅ Know your market value

### Later Career (Years 13+)
❌ Resisting new technologies
❌ Not building a brand
❌ Burning bridges
❌ Neglecting network
❌ Losing technical edge

✅ Stay technically current
✅ Share knowledge publicly
✅ Maintain relationships
✅ Build your network
✅ Continuous skill development
