## Getting Started

### Complete Beginners
If you're new to tech:
1. Read the [Software Engineering guide](/docs/software-engineering) first
2. Choose between frontend, backend, or full-stack
3. Follow the beginner roadmap  
4. Build 3-5 portfolio projects
5. Apply to junior positions

### Career Switchers
If you're transitioning from another field:
1. Leverage your existing skills
2. Choose a path that aligns with your background
3. Focus on building a strong portfolio
4. Network with professionals in your target role
5. Consider bootcamps or structured programs

### Advancing Professionals
If you're looking to level up:
1. Identify skill gaps for your next role
2. Take on stretch projects
3. Build leadership experience
4. Specialize in high-demand areas
5. Network strategically
